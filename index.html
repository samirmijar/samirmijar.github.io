My story

From Chains To Purpose

I'm sharing my full story because I believe that if my darkest moments can help even one person find the light, every hardship was worth it.

Before: A Childhood In The Shadows

I come from a deeply impoverished and struggling family. Decades ago, during the Panchayat era, my grandfather and father suffered unimaginable pain — the army confiscated our land and property, destroying twenty-eight houses in our village. Stripped of everything and lacking formal education, my father survived by working as a merchant, selling turmeric just to put food in our mouths. Surrounded by this poverty, our family was also deeply trapped in superstition and shamanism. As a child I was constantly sick, dragged from one shaman to another, forced to pour water on Peepal trees in a desperate search for healing. My father suffered severe mental breakdowns, and our home lacked peace. But about twelve years ago, after a severe crisis put my father in the hospital, he became the first believer in our house. Six months later, our entire family followed. I was only eleven or twelve and didn't fully understand who Jesus was — I just loved going to church to sing and praise.

Psalm 34:18 — The Lord is close to the brokenhearted.

Lost Years: Fear, Isolation And Addiction

Despite my family finding faith, my teenage years were a battlefield. I was the quietest one in school — an extreme introvert who struggled to speak, painfully shy, crippled by fear, with no confidence and no vision for my future. After passing the 10th grade and entering college, I started working online — providing digital solutions, managing social media, designing logos, and selling gift cards and top-ups. I started earning money, but without wisdom that money became a trap. I fell into a cycle of modern addictions: online gaming, pornography, masturbation, and endless hours lost to movies and mobile reels. Worse, I fell into online gambling, mistaking it for investment, and lost the money I had worked so hard to earn. These distractions completely stalled my walk with God. I had no friends, and I carried that weight alone. I would try to pray, fall back into the same cycle, and try again. Nobody understood the silent weight I was carrying.

2 Timothy 1:7 — God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love and a sound mind.

The Turning Point: Meeting The Truth

The turning point came when I realized these digital escapes were destroying my peace, my happiness, and my life. I started seeking God desperately — reading my Bible and watching videos on how to break free from addiction. Jesus met me exactly where I was and healed me step by step. He became my actual Friend — the boy who had no friends and couldn't speak to anyone found his ultimate confidant in God. I finally understood the truth of John 8:32: "The truth will set you free." When the Holy Spirit began working in my life, He gave me the vision I had lacked my entire youth.

John 8:32 — The truth will set you free. 2 Corinthians 5:17 — If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; the old has gone, the new has come.

Today: Surrendered To Jesus

Looking back, I do not regret a single part of my past. Every failure and every lost dollar was a lesson God used to teach me discipline and dependence on Him. As I step into my twenty-fourth year, I now live by a set of principles to protect my mind and honor God, anchored by Matthew 6:33 — seeking His kingdom first:

  • Put God first in everything.
  • Build physical and mental discipline.
  • Learn the power of saying "No."
  • Forgive the past — and forgive your father.
  • Build your business and personal brand with integrity.
  • Never abandon your family.
  • Never, ever give up.

Today, God has removed my fear and replaced it with His confidence. He has given me the incredible privilege to simply serve in my church. I know He is still preparing me for His kingdom. My life is for Jesus, and I will spread heavenly love wherever I go. God always answers our prayers — He takes the broken and the addicted and turns them into warriors for His kingdom.

Matthew 6:33 — Seek first His kingdom. Revelation 12:11 — They triumphed by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony.

Jesus is coming soon — repent and seek God.

If you see yourself in my addictions, my fear, or my family's brokenness — know that the same God who found me in the dark can find you too. Your past is not your final chapter.

Philippians 4:13 — I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.
The good news

The Gospel of Jesus Christ

The heart of the Christian message is simple: God loves us, Jesus saves, and we are invited to follow Him.

01

God Created You

You are not an accident. God made you in His image and loves you deeply (Genesis 1:27; Jeremiah 29:11).

02

Sin Separates Us

Everyone has sinned and falls short of God's standard, and sin separates us from a holy God (Romans 3:23).

03

The Price of Sin

The wages of sin is death, but God offers eternal life as a free gift through Jesus Christ (Romans 6:23).

04

Jesus Died For You

While we were still sinners, Christ died for us. His death paid a price we could never pay ourselves (Romans 5:8).

05

Jesus Rose Again

On the third day Jesus rose from the dead, defeating sin and death and proving He is the Son of God (1 Corinthians 15:3-4).

06

New Life In Him

If you believe in your heart and confess Jesus as Lord, you will be saved and receive eternal life (Romans 10:9).

Your next step

Ready to follow Jesus?

Wherever you are in the world, God is near, and this step is simple:

1

Admit — Recognize that you have sinned and need God's forgiveness.

2

Believe — Trust that Jesus died for your sins and rose again.

3

Confess — Speak to God in prayer and receive Jesus as your Lord and Savior.

A simple prayer

Dear God, I know I have sinned and need Your forgiveness. I believe Jesus died for me and rose again. I turn to You now and receive Jesus as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for loving me and giving me new life. In Jesus' name, Amen.

If you prayed this prayer and meant it, you have taken the first step of faith. Reach out — I would love to encourage you and help with your next step.
